premiums, the statutory mandatory excess/eigen risico, wheelchair, mobility
scooter, and home modification are all excluded from deductible 2025 specific
healthcare costs. (2) Heffingskortingen reduce the combined levy
(`gecombineerde heffing`: income tax plus national-insurance contributions),
not box-1 income tax alone. (3) The invitation letter controls the deadline; on
the normal online route extension is requested before 1 May 2026 and normally
runs to 1 September 2026; a letter with another date uses that date and the
official form route; with no invitation extension is unavailable, while the
14 July 2026 guardrail applies only when the taxpayer establishes tax is due.
Fail if the response collapses these into one unconditional deadline.
