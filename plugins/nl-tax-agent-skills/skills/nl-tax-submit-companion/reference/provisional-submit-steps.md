# Manual Submission Steps -- Voorlopige Aanslag 2026

> **HUMAN-ONLY PORTAL STEPS.** You (the taxpayer) or an authorized human
> perform every step below on your own device. The assistant must not open or
> operate Mijn Belastingdienst, enter values, sign, send, or submit.

On the real portal, **requesting and changing** a voorlopige aanslag share one
combined entry ("Voorlopige aanslag aanvragen of wijzigen"), and the form runs in
four steps: **Voorbereiden -> Inloggen -> Invullen -> Ondertekenen en verzenden**.
When you open the form the portal may offer to **pre-fill (vooraf invullen)** it
from your most recent aangifte (the data from two years prior). Accepting the
pre-fill is fine -- you still verify and complete every field against the
Field map summary. Describe the navigation generically; the exact tile wording
can change, so match it to whatever the live portal shows under "Voorlopige
aanslag".

## For REQUEST (aanvragen)

1. **You (the taxpayer):** Go to Mijn Belastingdienst (mijn.belastingdienst.nl).
2. **You (the taxpayer):** Log in to Mijn Belastingdienst.
3. **You (the taxpayer):** Open "Voorlopige aanslag aanvragen of wijzigen" and choose to request (aanvragen) for 2026.
4. **You (the taxpayer):** If offered, accept the pre-fill (vooraf invullen) from the prior aangifte, then verify each value against the Field map summary.
5. **You (the taxpayer):** Enter or confirm estimated income using the Field map summary.
6. **You (the taxpayer):** Enter or confirm deductions and box 3 estimates.
7. **You (the taxpayer):** Review the summary.
8. **You (the taxpayer):** Sign and send (ondertekenen en verzenden).
9. **You (the taxpayer):** Note the response timing shown in Mijn Belastingdienst (typically within about 4 weeks, up to 8 weeks).

## For CHANGE (wijzigen)

1. **You (the taxpayer):** Go to Mijn Belastingdienst (mijn.belastingdienst.nl).
2. **You (the taxpayer):** Log in to Mijn Belastingdienst.
3. **You (the taxpayer):** Open "Voorlopige aanslag aanvragen of wijzigen" and choose to change (wijzigen) the existing 2026 voorlopige aanslag.
4. **You (the taxpayer):** If offered, accept the pre-fill (vooraf invullen); the form may already show prior-year figures, but the change replaces the old beschikking entirely.
5. **You (the taxpayer):** Enter and verify the complete dataset using the Field map summary; the change form requires all applicable categories, not only the changed item.
6. **You (the taxpayer):** Review the summary.
7. **You (the taxpayer):** Sign and send (ondertekenen en verzenden).
8. **You (the taxpayer):** Save the new beschikking when it arrives and review the adjusted monthly amounts.

**Important:** Whether the form opens blank or pre-filled, prepare and verify the
complete dataset. The change form requires all applicable categories, not only
the changed item.
