# Manual Steps -- Stopping a Voorlopige Aanslag (Stopzetten)

The official form is **"Uitbetaling van mijn voorlopige aanslag stopzetten"**,
found under Inkomstenbelasting in Mijn Belastingdienst. It is only visible once
the voorlopige aanslag (the monthly refund) has actually been issued.

**Hard deadline: stopzetten for 2026 must be done before 1 October 2026.** After
that the refund keeps running for the year and is settled at the annual return.
Before writing checklist steps, compare the current date with 2026-10-01. If the
current date is on or after 2026-10-01, do not generate a stopzetten checklist;
put the passed cutoff in Blockers and route to review/change or annual-return
settlement.

## Steps

1. Go to Mijn Belastingdienst (mijn.belastingdienst.nl)
2. Log in to Mijn Belastingdienst
3. Under Inkomstenbelasting, open the form "Uitbetaling van mijn voorlopige aanslag stopzetten" for 2026 (visible only after the aanslag has been received)
4. Confirm that you want to stop the monthly refund -- do this before 1 October 2026
5. Monthly refunds will stop
6. Final settlement happens at annual return time

## Note

Stopzetten is for taxpayers who **receive** a monthly refund (teruggaaf). If you
currently **pay** monthly and want a different amount, use the CHANGE (wijzigen)
process instead of stopzetten -- stopping payments does not reduce the underlying
debt. Changing recalculates the provisional assessment from current estimates.
