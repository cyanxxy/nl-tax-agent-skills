## Phase 9 — Open questions and human review

### 9.1 Open questions

List every question still awaiting the taxpayer in the workpack's
`## Open questions` section: stable Q-ID, section key, question, blocking
yes/no, and open or deferred. Include areas of uncertainty such as:
- "Can you confirm the WOZ-waarde on your beschikking is EUR [amount]?"
- "Did you receive any income from other sources not yet mentioned?"
- "Do you have the actual interest statements from your bank for 2025?"

Order questions by impact on the return:
1. Items that affect whether filing is possible
2. Items that affect the tax amount significantly
3. Items that affect accuracy but have smaller impact

The Q-IDs in this section must equal the `open` lists in Appendix A. A blocking
open question keeps the workpack a draft.

### 9.2 Human review checklist

Carry into the workpack's `## Human review checklist` every point the taxpayer
must verify personally before filing: each `U:` value, each accepted
assumption, each `Documents and sources` row marked `needs review`, each stale
source flagged by the freshness check, and each manual-review routing
outcome. Phrase every item for the taxpayer or an authorized human, never as an
action for the assistant.
