## Scope

Use these notes for standard full-year Dutch resident annual 2025 preparation
workpacks. Outputs are for manual Mijn Belastingdienst entry and review only.

## Substantial Interest

- A substantial interest generally starts when the taxpayer, directly or
  indirectly and together with a fiscal partner, owns at least 5% of shares,
  profit-sharing certificates, options, or similar rights in a company.
- If the holding percentage, rights class, option position, or partner ownership
  chain is unclear, route to manual review.

## Rates

| 2025 taxable Box 2 income | Rate |
|---|---:|
| Up to EUR 67,804 | 24.5% |
| Above EUR 67,804 | 31.0% |

## Regular Benefits

- Regular benefits include dividends and similar distributions from the
  substantial-interest company.
- Deductible costs directly connected with regular benefits reduce Box 2
  income, but uncertain or mixed costs need review.
- Dutch dividend withholding tax connected with the Box 2 dividend can be
  credited in the same year in the preparation calculation.

## Disposal Benefits

- Disposal benefits include gains from selling or transferring substantial
  interest shares or rights.
- Calculate the disposal benefit as the official net disposal or transfer price
  minus acquisition price.
- A negative disposal result is loss data and needs manual review before entry.

## Acquisition Price

- Use the taxpayer's acquisition price or fiscally recognized acquisition value
  for the disposed substantial-interest shares or rights.
- Review is needed for inherited or gifted interests, mergers, demergers,
  share-for-share exchanges, informal capital, step-up questions, or historic
  values that are not evidenced.

## Disposal Or Transfer Price

- Use the actual net disposal or transfer price from the evidence for
  arm's-length transfers. If evidence starts from gross sale proceeds, subtract
  disposal or transfer costs once to derive the net transfer price first.
- Non-arm's-length transfers, valuation disputes, transfers to related parties,
  or uncertain market values are outside the standard helper boundary.

## Deductible Costs

- Include only costs that directly relate to receiving regular benefits or
  realizing a disposal benefit.
- Do not subtract disposal or transfer costs twice: the official transfer price
  is already net of those costs.
- Legal, advisory, financing, or mixed DGA costs require review when the link to
  Box 2 is not clear.

## Dividend Withholding Tax

- Dutch dividend withholding tax can be included as a credit against the
  same-year Box 2 preparation tax amount.
- If the credit exceeds gross Box 2 tax, present the result as
  refund-facing review data and route for review rather than final advice.

## Loss Handling

- Negative Box 2 income or loss setoff should be recorded as manual-review data.
- Do not decide carry-back, carry-forward, or formal loss-determination issues
  in this helper.

## Fiscal Partner Allocation

- Full-year fiscal partners may allocate Box 2 income in any split totaling
  100%.
- Validate the selected percentages before calculation and show each partner's
  allocated income and preparation tax amount separately.

## Excessive Borrowing From Own BV

- Borrowing above EUR 500,000 from the own BV can create a fictitious regular
  benefit.
- Include a structured fictitious regular benefit only when supplied by the
  evidence or reviewer, and always flag it for manual review.

## Complex Or Manual-Review Markers

Route the case out of the standard helper path when any of these are present:

- Valuation dispute.
- Emigration, immigration, treaty, nonresident, or partial-year resident issue.
- Death during the year.
- Restructuring, merger, demerger, share-for-share exchange, or conversion.
- Informal capital or shareholder contribution issue.
- Non-arm's-length transfer or related-party pricing uncertainty.
- Corporate-tax-heavy DGA case.
- Inherited or gifted substantial interest.
- Fictive disposal event.
- Excessive-borrowing uncertainty.
