## Scope

Use these notes for `provisional_2026` Box 2 estimate support. Every amount in
the workpack must be labeled as an estimate or baseline-derived amount for
manual provisional-assessment entry.

## Substantial-Interest Baseline

- Baseline-derived substantial-interest status generally starts at a direct or
  indirect holding of at least 5%, checked together with the fiscal partner.
- If the 2026 holding percentage, rights class, option position, or ownership
  chain is uncertain, mark the item for manual review instead of treating it as
  standard.

## Estimated Regular Benefits

- Estimated regular benefits include expected dividends and similar
  distributions from the substantial-interest company.
- Baseline-derived deductible costs may reduce estimated regular benefits only
  when the link to Box 2 is clear.
- Estimated Dutch dividend withholding tax may be shown as a same-year credit
  in the provisional preparation calculation.

## Estimated Disposal Benefits

- Include a 2026 estimated disposal benefit only when the taxpayer expects a
  sale or transfer of substantial-interest shares or rights.
- Estimate the disposal benefit as estimated net disposal or transfer price
  minus baseline-derived acquisition price. If the estimate starts from gross
  proceeds, subtract estimated disposal costs once to derive the net transfer
  price first.
- If the expected transfer price or acquisition price is uncertain, route to
  manual review.

## Baseline-Derived Acquisition Price

- Use a baseline-derived acquisition price only when supported by prior records
  or evidence supplied for the provisional estimate.
- Inherited or gifted interests, restructurings, historic value questions, and
  informal-capital issues are outside the standard estimate path.

## Estimated Disposal Or Transfer Price

- Use an estimated net disposal or transfer price only for expected
  arm's-length transactions.
- Related-party pricing, valuation disputes, or non-arm's-length estimates
  require manual review.

## Estimated Deductible Costs

- Include only estimated costs directly connected with expected regular benefits
  or an expected disposal benefit.
- Do not subtract estimated disposal costs twice: the transfer price used for
  the Box 2 benefit should be net of those costs.
- Mixed DGA costs, legal disputes, financing questions, and advisory costs need
  review when the Box 2 link is not clear.

## Estimated Dividend Withholding Tax

- Estimated Dutch dividend withholding tax may be included as a credit in the
  provisional estimate.
- If the estimated credit exceeds estimated gross Box 2 tax, label the net
  amount as refund-facing review data and mark for review.

## Estimated Loss And Setoff Data

- Negative estimated Box 2 income and baseline-derived loss setoff are
  manual-review data.
- Do not decide formal loss availability, ordering, or entitlement inside this
  helper.

## Fiscal Partner Allocation Estimate

- Full-year fiscal partners may choose an estimated allocation split that totals
  100%.
- Show each partner's estimated allocated income and preparation tax amount separately.

## Excessive Borrowing Estimate

- Borrowing above the EUR 500,000 baseline from the own BV can create a
  fictitious regular benefit in 2026.
- Always treat this as manual-review data because prior-year threshold
  adjustments, repayments, and home-loan exceptions can change the usable
  amount.
- Do not calculate a fictitious regular benefit from BV lending in provisional
  2026 unless a reviewer supplies the amount and source.

## Complex Or Manual-Review Markers

For provisional 2026, route out of standard helper handling when any estimate
involves:

- Valuation dispute.
- Emigration, immigration, treaty, nonresident, or partial-year resident issue.
- Death during the year.
- Restructuring, merger, demerger, share-for-share exchange, or conversion.
- Informal capital or shareholder contribution issue.
- Non-arm's-length transfer or related-party pricing uncertainty.
- Corporate-tax-heavy DGA case.
- Inherited or gifted substantial interest.
- Fictive disposal event.
- Excessive-borrowing uncertainty.
