The public 2026 Fisin international guidance was researched on 2 October
2026. Human source-content review and an exact final 2026 M/C annual form
inventory are not established by this research. This skill supports dated
evidence collection and arithmetic reconciliation only, for a taxpayer whose
2026 aangifte inkomstenbelasting will be a migration return (M-biljet, for
emigratie or immigratie during 2026) or a nonresident return (C-biljet, for
a buitenlands belastingplichtige for the whole of 2026).

## Year-specific evidence

The 2026 guidance still separates tax liability, qualifying nonresident
conditions and mandatory-insurance liability. It describes the residence
country and worldwide-income test, while its income-statement passage differs
from the 2025 guidance and says the condition is abolished from 2026.
Do not copy a 2025 statement requirement into 2026 or regard an earlier
qualification answer as current eligibility. Keep year-specific proof and
deduction treatment for human source-content review.

The official partial-foreign-tax-liability page says that 2026 is the last
year in which a taxpayer who used the expat ruling (30%-regeling) before 2024
can, under transitional law, choose partial foreign tax liability
(partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) for Box 2 and Box 3. For a 2026
resident period record only the facts: whether an expat-ruling decision
(30%-beschikking) existed, the dates it applied and whether the taxpayer
intends to make the choice. The effect on the 2026 annual form stays a named
review item pending the 2026 annual form review.

For a 2026 emigration, record the existence and type of pension or lijfrente
aanspraken, eigen-woning capital insurance and substantial-interest shares at
the emigration date as evidence only. Do not reuse the 2025 conserved-income
question number as a 2026 field; the 2026 treatment stays a named review item.

## Preparation boundary

Collect exact residence/work/insurance intervals, country/category income,
withholding, asset/debt dates, treaty evidence, deductions and partner facts.
Distinguish actual records to date, later estimates and unreceived year-end
statements; do not turn estimates into annual actuals. A confirmed group sum
or reconciliation difference may be shown as preparation arithmetic.

Always retain `2026 annual international schema review` and draft readiness.
Do not assert final M/C filing availability, reproduce 2025 screen labels as
2026 fields, calculate qualification/final tax from 2025 rules or produce a
manual-entry checklist. The mapper may show collected facts and missing/review
rows, but no invented final annual entry rows. A provisional assessment is a
separate workflow with its own sources and consent.
