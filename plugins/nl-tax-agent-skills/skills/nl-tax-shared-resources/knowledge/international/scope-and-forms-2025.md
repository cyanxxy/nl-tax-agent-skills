This note covers the 2025 aangifte inkomstenbelasting for a migration year
(aangifte M) and for a nonresident (aangifte C). These official public
sources were researched on 2 October 2026. Human
tax-content and exact form-schema review remain outstanding. Use this note
for draft preparation, not a final liability or form-entry instruction.

## Return and filing basis

The M route (aangifte M, often called the M-biljet) covers the year of
immigration (immigratie) or emigration (emigratie); the human may file
online or use the paper M form. An entrepreneur using paper M also needs
M annual business statements. The C route (aangifte C, often called the
C-biljet) covers a full year of foreign residence as a buitenlands
belastingplichtige with relevant Dutch income or assets; it is available
online and on paper. Confirm the actual residence facts rather than choosing
from citizenship.

For C, an invitation requires filing. Without a letter, the official 2025
page gives an assessment boundary of EUR 57 and refund boundary of EUR 18,
and describes filing where the amount payable/refundable exceeds its boundary.
The public five-year window is not a substitute for an invitation's deadline.
Record the actual invitation/extension, or keep the filing obligation open.
The assistant cannot open the official return to establish the outcome.

For a C 2025 return the taxpayer requested personally (for example to claim
a refund), the 2025 C explanation says it must be received before
1 January 2030, while the official C-return page (bd_intl_c_return) lists
31 December 2030 as the latest filing date for 2025. The two official
sources disagree by one year. Record both dates, advise the human to file
well before the earlier date, and keep the exact last date as a named
human-confirmation item (C 2025 self-requested-return last date).

The 2025 C explanation says that belastingrente can apply when the return is
filed after 1 May 2026 and refers to the website for the detailed rules. A
refund alone does not establish a right to interest. The specific refund
guidance (bd_intl_refund_interest, checked on 4 October 2026) requires all of
the following when a refund assessment is issued: tax is refunded, the return
is accepted unchanged, the Belastingdienst took more than 13 weeks after
receiving the return to issue the assessment, and the assessment was issued
after 1 July following the tax year (1 July 2026 for a 2025 return). A refund
assessed within 13 weeks therefore does not qualify merely because the return
was filed after 1 May. Reductions of provisional assessments have separate
conditions in that guidance; distinguish that situation before advising.
Do not calculate or promise belastingrente. Explain that it may apply and
identify missing dates or conditions. No equivalent
dated belastingrente statement for M 2025 was found in the M explanation;
do not transfer the C statement to an M return.

## Exact residence and preparation periods

The 2025 M explanation separates periods living in and outside the
Netherlands. Temporary foreign presence can coexist with Dutch tax residence;
family/home ties and official determination matter. Government postings may
require another return. Uncertain tax domicile needs authority/adviser review.

Keep exact residence dates separate from form-specific full-month counts:
different M calculations use different period conventions. Do not allocate
salary, credits or Box 3 by one generic day ratio. Collect actual income by
period, applicable valuation dates, and both fictitious and actual-return
(werkelijk rendement) Box 3 evidence.

The 2025 M and C explanations deduct the per-person heffingsvrij vermogen in
full in each Box 3 computation; the partner amount applies only under the
partner conditions each explanation states. Any month-based scaling of a Box 3
benefit applies only where the exact form's rekenhulp prescribes it.

## Currency conversion (2025)

Both the M and the C explanation convert income and expenses at the bank
exchange rate on the date the income or expense occurred, never at the
preparation date. The C explanation calls this the bankkoers (middenkoers);
the M explanation refers to the website of the European Central Bank (ECB)
to look up the bank rate. For Box 3 items both explanations use the ECB rate
on the relevant dates: the valuation dates (1 January or a later immigration
date; 31 December or an earlier emigration date), the purchase, sale or
payment days, and for dividends the day of receipt. Where the explanation
allows it, a monthly ECB rate may replace the day rate. Record per item the
original amount, currency, rate, rate date, rate source and EUR derivation.
An unresolved conversion or period treatment remains a draft gap.

## Partial foreign tax liability (30%-bewijsregel)

From 1 January 2025 a resident can no longer choose partial foreign tax
liability (partiële buitenlandse belastingplicht) in the return. A taxpayer
who used the expat ruling (30%-regeling) before 2024 can, under transitional
law, still use the choice up to and including 2026. With the choice, the
resident is treated as a buitenlands belastingplichtige for Box 2 and Box 3
during the period in which the expat ruling applies to the wages. The
official page lists the Box 3 assets that are then still reported: real
estate in the Netherlands, rights relating to real estate in the
Netherlands, and a profit right in a Dutch enterprise, plus the debts that
belong to those assets. For Box 2 only income from a substantial interest in
a company or cooperative established in the Netherlands is reported. The
2025 M explanation applies this to the resident period (binnenlandse
periode) by limiting Box 3 assets to questions 32g and 32k, with the
related debts at questions 33a and 33c. If the expat ruling stops during the
year, the choice applies only up to that date. The choice can be made until
the assessment is final (onherroepelijk).

Ask whether an expat-ruling decision (30%-beschikking) existed for the
period and whether the taxpayer makes the choice. Eligibility, the dates the
ruling applied, partner treatment and the choice itself are named review
items: never apply the choice by default and never reject it from memory.
While it is unresolved, keep foreign Box 2 and Box 3 items in the worldwide
evidence inventory, not as Dutch-reportable amounts.

## Conserved income and revisierente (te conserveren inkomen)

The 2025 M explanation (question 95) says that every taxpayer who emigrated
from the Netherlands must always answer the te conserveren inkomen question.
Conserved income leads to a separate conserverende aanslag. A pension or
lijfrente aanspraak at emigration almost always produces one. The question
also covers pension or lijfrente capital moved to a foreign insurer during
2025, a kapitaalverzekering, spaarrekening or beleggingsrecht eigen woning
whose value at emigration exceeds its exemption, and an aanmerkelijk belang
held at the emigration date (treated as a fictitious disposal). Whether the
value in economic transactions (waarde economisch verkeer) or the paid
premiums applies depends on the applicable tax treaty. The explanation also
says that filing an M return counts as a request for deferral of payment
(uitstel van betaling) of the conserverende aanslag, and that emigration to
an EU or EEA member state in 2025 gives unconditional and automatic deferral.

Collect the existence, type and provider category of each such position at
the emigration date, without policy or contract numbers. Valuation, treaty
basis and the deferral outcome remain named review items. Do not calculate
the conserverende aanslag. A pension or lijfrente buy-out (afkoop) or another
breach of its conditions in 2025 can also trigger revisierente (question 94),
for which the explanation gives a tegenbewijsregeling; record the event and
leave the amount for human review.

These questions are not limited to emigration. The 2025 C explanation also
asks nonresidents about revisierente (question 59) and te conserveren
inkomen (question 60). Question 60 covers a lijfrente that continues with a
non-admitted foreign insurer after immigration, a pension or lijfrente moved
to a foreign insurer in connection with international work, onward emigration
((door)emigratie), and a substantial interest acquired by a nonresident. The
2025 M explanation's questions 95c, 95e and 95f cover pension or lijfrente
capital placed with a foreign insurer during 2025, in the resident or the
nonresident period, so they also apply in an immigration year. Revisierente
(M question 94) applies to any 2025 pension or lijfrente buy-out or breach of
its conditions, not only on emigration. For an immigration-year M return and
for every C return, ask each of these items; an item is not applicable only
with a sourced answer for that item.
