This note supports preparing the resident annual income-tax return for 2026
(aangifte inkomstenbelasting 2026). Public research on 2 October 2026 supports
draft preparation only; no human content review or final annual screen review
is recorded. The year is still open. Collect actual coverage and pending
year-end statements, such as the jaaropgaaf from each employer or benefit
payer and year-end bank and debt statements. A final annual return needs
actual full-year facts and the exact annual schema. No filing-season opening
or personal deadline is established here. Provisional 2026 estimates and the
reviewed resident 2025 schema cannot substitute for annual 2026 facts or
schema.

## Income, business and evidence

Use the 2026 Fiscale informatie topics to collect wages, pension, benefits,
other-work income, business profit-and-loss and balance sheet, own home, Box 2,
Box 3, deductions, credits and withholding. Ordinary sole-trader draft profit
must reconcile the profit-and-loss account, opening and closing capital,
private movements, investments and tax adjustments. Keep entrepreneurship,
hours and prior deduction decisions separate from VAT status. Treaties,
special business valuations or reserves, and social insurance require
topic-specific sources and review.

For 2026 the ordinary zelfstandigenaftrek is EUR 1,200 (EUR 600 if AOW age was
reached at the start of the year). The startersaftrek for 2026 is EUR 2,123
(EUR 1,062 if AOW age was reached at the start of the year), subject to the
starter conditions: the taxpayer was not an entrepreneur in one or more of the
5 preceding calendar years and applied the zelfstandigenaftrek no more than 2
times in that period. The zelfstandigenaftrek cannot exceed profit before
ondernemersaftrek unless the startersaftrek applies. Profit limitation, the
hours test (urencriterium), the starter conditions and prior unrealised
deduction decisions (niet-gerealiseerde zelfstandigenaftrek) must each be
tested against evidence. The MKB exemption (MKB-winstvrijstelling) is 12.7% of
profit after ondernemersaftrek; it also reduces a loss. The tariefsaanpassing
(art. 2.10 lid 2 and lid 3 Wet IB 2001) adds 11.94% of the amount by which
taxable box 1 income plus the deducted grondslagverminderende posten exceeds
EUR 78,426, capped at 11.94% of those posts, which limits their benefit to
37.56%. The posts are the ondernemersaftrek (always, including the
zelfstandigenaftrek and the startersaftrek), the MKB exemption (only when the
profit after ondernemersaftrek is positive), the
terbeschikkingstellingsvrijstelling (only when the result from those
activities is positive), the deductible eigen-woning costs and the
persoonsgebonden aftrek. Do not apply the MKB
exemption or the zelfstandigenaftrek to medegerechtigde income. These are
staged rules, not permission to apply every business deduction without its
exact evidence.

The normal Box 1 bands for a taxpayer who does not reach AOW age in 2026 are
EUR 38,883 and EUR 78,426, with rates of 35.75%, 37.56% and 49.50%. For a
taxpayer who reached AOW age before 2026 and was born on or after 1 January
1946, the rates are 17.85% up to EUR 38,883, 37.56% up to EUR 78,426 and 49.50%
above that (bd_annual2026_box1_rates). For a taxpayer born before 1 January
1946, the rates are 17.85% up to EUR 41,123, 37.56% up to EUR 78,426 and 49.50%
above that. For a taxpayer who reaches AOW age during 2026, the first-band rate
depends on the month in which AOW age is reached, from 17.85% (January) to
34.25% (December) according to the belastingberekening month series; the
second and third bands are 37.56% and 49.50%. Take the exact month rate from
that series and record the month as a sourced household fact. The two official
pages differ by 0.01 percentage point for some months (the box 1 page shows
34.26% for December); the belastingberekening series is the one used here, and
the official filing environment is binding. The belastingberekening table
headed 'geboren op of na 1 januari 1946' under 'AOW-leeftijd al bereikt'
prints 35.75% for the first band; that figure is the non-AOW rate and must
never be used for a taxpayer who has reached AOW age. Withholding, credits,
deductible-rate caps and Zvw remain separate from gross band tax. No final
assessment computation is enabled by this note alone.

## Box 2 and Box 3: transitional partial foreign tax liability

The choice for partial foreign tax liability (partiële buitenlandse
belastingplicht) ended from 1 January 2025. Under transitional law, a resident
taxpayer who used the expat ruling (30%-regeling) before 2024 can still use it
up to and including 2026, so 2026 is the last year it is available. The choice
can only apply while the expat ruling runs; if the ruling stops during 2026,
it applies only up to that date. A taxpayer who makes the choice answers the
Box 2 and Box 3 questions as a nonresident taxpayer: Box 3 then contains only
Dutch real estate, rights relating to Dutch real estate and a profit right in a
Dutch enterprise, with only the related debts, and Box 2 contains only income
from a substantial interest in a company or cooperative established in the
Netherlands. There is no separate form; the choice is made in the annual
return and can be passed on until the assessment is final
(bd_annual2026_partial_foreign_liability). Ask about it before collecting
foreign Box 2 or Box 3 items as Dutch-reportable amounts. Eligibility and the
choice are a named human-review item; never apply the choice by default.

## Box 3: distinguish annual evidence and forecasts

Collect 1 January assets and debts and the full-year actual income, capital
movements and value changes; missing 31 December values remain open. The
actual return (werkelijk rendement, also called tegenbewijs) covers the total
portfolio, with no tax-free-capital exemption and with losses within the year
set off; a negative total is floored at zero, with no loss carry to other
years. Ordinary costs are not deducted; Box 3 debt interest and the specific
qualifying property investment rule need separate evidence. Never treat
capital contributions as investment return and never omit unclosed accounts.
Reporting the actual return is optional; when the taxpayer reports it, the
official filing environment uses the more favourable of the fictitious
(forfaitair) return and the actual return.

From 2026, own use of a second home or other Box 3 property adds a benefit
(bijtelling eigen gebruik) to the actual return (werkelijk rendement or
tegenbewijs) only; it is never added to the fictitious (forfaitair) Box 3
return. Collect rental, unusable and available-for-own-use days, including days
on which the owner is not present. The taxpayer chooses the method: the
economic annual rental value, or 5.06% of the applicable WOZ value (the
valuation date for 2026 is 1 January 2025), prorated for the days the property
is available for own use. For a 365-day year the 5.06% forfait is WOZ value
multiplied by 0.0506 multiplied by available days divided by 365. Document
ownership fractions and effective dates; foreign property or a special
valuation requires its own source. This addition must not be imported into
2025.

The current final annual forfait table ends at 2025; another 2026 information
page displays figures also used by provisional assessment. These do not prove
the final 2026 savings and debt percentages. Preserve that distinction and
block a final annual comparison until reviewed final values and schema exist.
Do not copy 2025 values or call provisional 2026 rates final.
