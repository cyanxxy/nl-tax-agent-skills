## Rule

The Dutch income tax system provides several deduction categories that reduce taxable income. This note covers the main deductions relevant to the 2025 annual return. Most deductions fall under the persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions) or are specific deductions within box 1.

These are reference notes for workpack preparation -- not final tax advice.

## Persoonsgebonden aftrek (personal deductions)

The persoonsgebonden aftrek is a collection of deductible items that are subtracted from total income. They are first applied to box 1, then box 3, then box 2 income (in that order). The main components are:

### Alimentatie (maintenance payments to ex-partner)

- Qualifying partneralimentatie paid to an ex-partner is deductible (subject to
  the general high-income deduction-rate limitation described below).
- Kinderalimentatie (child maintenance) is NOT deductible.
- A court order, divorce agreement, or notarial deed can establish the
  obligation, but it is not the only possible basis. Payments arising from an
  urgent moral obligation can also qualify when that obligation is legally
  enforceable—for example in some post-cohabitation support situations.
- The recipient must report the alimentatie received as box 1 income.
- Collect the instrument/agreement or facts supporting the enforceable
  obligation, plus proof of the payments made. If the legal enforceability or
  payment category is unclear, do not reject or claim it automatically; keep it
  as a manual-review question.

### Specifieke zorgkosten (specific healthcare costs)

Medical expenses that are not reimbursed by health insurance may be deductible, but only the portion that exceeds a drempel (threshold).

#### Potentially qualifying expenses to inventory

- Prescribed medication (voorgeschreven medicijnen) not covered by basic insurance
- Medical aids and devices (hulpmiddelen) such as hearing aids and prosthetics
- Extra costs of a medically prescribed diet (dieetkosten) -- standard amounts per diet type set by the Belastingdienst
- Travel by car to arts, ziekenhuis, or apotheek: EUR 0.23 per kilometer (parking, ferry, and toll costs separately). Travel by taxi or public transport: actual costs.
- Reiskosten ziekenbezoek (visiting a sick relative): EUR 0.23 per km, but only if (a) you shared a household with the sick person at the start of the illness, (b) the verpleging lasts longer than 1 month in total, and (c) the one-way distance home-to-place-of-care exceeds 10 km
- Extra clothing and bedding costs due to illness or disability (extra kleding en beddengoed)
- Prescribed therapies not covered by insurance

Wheelchair: not deductible. Scooters and home modifications are also not
deductible healthcare costs for 2025. Reimbursed costs, health-insurance
premiums, and the statutory excess (verplicht eigen risico) are not included.
The agent inventories evidence that may qualify; it does not treat the inventory
as a completed deduction calculation.

#### Threshold (drempel)

- Drempelinkomen is the total income in boxes 1, 2, and 3 before
  persoonsgebonden aftrek. Use the combined amount when the taxpayer had a
  fiscal partner for all of 2025 or elects full-year partner treatment.
- Without a full-year fiscal partner, the 2025 drempel is:

  | Drempelinkomen | Drempel |
  |---|---:|
  | EUR 0 through EUR 9,534 | EUR 164 |
  | above EUR 9,534 through EUR 50,635 | 1.65% of drempelinkomen |
  | above EUR 50,635 | EUR 835 + 5.75% of the amount above EUR 50,635 |

- With a full-year fiscal partner, use the combined drempelinkomen and this
  table:

  | Combined drempelinkomen | Drempel |
  |---|---:|
  | EUR 0 through EUR 19,068 | EUR 328 |
  | above EUR 19,068 through EUR 50,635 | 1.65% of combined drempelinkomen |
  | above EUR 50,635 | EUR 835 + 5.75% of the amount above EUR 50,635 |

- Apply the table only after the agent has established partner status and the
  complete drempelinkomen. If either is unresolved, show the missing input and
  leave the result for review in Mijn Belastingdienst.

#### Multiplier for specific costs

Before applying the drempel, increase the eligible subset of specific
healthcare costs when the taxpayer's (and any full-year partner's) combined
drempelinkomen is no more than EUR 40,502:

- add 40% when the taxpayer had not reached AOW age on 1 January 2025;
- add 113% when the taxpayer had reached AOW age on 1 January 2025;
- if one fiscal partner had reached AOW age and the other had not, use 113% for
  both partners' eligible costs.

Do not increase genees- en heelkundige hulp or reiskosten ziekenbezoek. The
agent must classify costs first, show the increased and non-increased subtotals
separately, and then subtract the drempel. Do not apply the percentage to the
entire healthcare-cost inventory.

#### Extra vervoerskosten: mobility forfait

- In 2025, a taxpayer who can substantiate that they cannot walk more than 100
  metres independently may include a fixed EUR 925 for extra transport caused
  by illness or disability.
- Possible evidence includes a disabled parking card, a WMO/PGB mobility
  decision or application, or a physician's statement.
- Subtract reimbursements received or available for these extra transport
  costs from EUR 925. Keep this distinct from medical-trip costs (EUR 0.23 per
  car kilometre, or actual taxi/public-transport costs).
- The agent asks about walking ability, evidence, and available reimbursements;
  it must not infer eligibility from a diagnosis alone.

### Giften (charitable donations)

Donations to ANBI-registered institutions (Algemeen Nut Beogende Instelling) and qualified cultural institutions (culturele ANBI) may be deductible. There are two categories:

#### Periodieke giften (periodic gifts)

- Regular donations committed for at least 5 years via a notarial deed or written agreement (schenkingsovereenkomst).
- No threshold (drempel) applies.
- Maximum deductible: **EUR 1.5 million** per year, combined for taxpayer and fiscal partner, for 2025 and 2026 (was EUR 250,000 in 2024).
- Transition rule (overgangsregeling): periodic-gift agreements entered into before 4 October 2022 at 16:00 keep the unlimited deduction up to 2027 ("tot 2027") — that is, the last full year with the unlimited (uncapped) deduction is tax year 2026; from tax year 2027 onward the EUR 1.5 million maximum applies to these agreements too. The agent records the agreement date and routes uncertain transition facts to manual review.
- Evidence required: notarial deed or written agreement, plus proof of payments.

#### Gewone giften / eenmalige giften (incidental gifts)

- One-time or irregular donations to ANBI institutions.
- Threshold (drempel): 1% of drempelinkomen (with a minimum of EUR 60).
- Maximum: 10% of drempelinkomen.
- Only the amount between the threshold and the maximum is deductible.
- Gifts to cultural ANBI institutions may be multiplied by 1.25 (up to a maximum increase of EUR 1,250) before applying the threshold and cap.

### Studiekosten / scholingsuitgaven (education expenses)

- Ordinary study and training expenses have not been deductible since 2022.
  Do not treat tuition, books, courses, or retraining paid in 2025 as a general
  deduction.
- Narrow exception: ask whether the taxpayer had a pre-1 July 2015 DUO
  prestatiebeurs whose diploma period expired in 2025 and which DUO definitively
  did not convert into a gift. If not, there is no 2025 studiekosten deduction.
- For the qualifying exception, use the lower of the prestatiebeurs amount and
  the fixed cap: EUR 1,693 per MBO study year (EUR 141.09 per month for part of
  a year), or EUR 2,443 per HBO/WO study year (EUR 203.59 per month).
- Apply a separate EUR 250 threshold to each partner's calculation. Afterwards,
  fiscal partners may combine the deductible results and allocate the total,
  provided their allocation totals 100%.
- Evidence: DUO notice showing the expired diploma period, the final conversion
  outcome, covered study periods, education level, and prestatiebeurs amount.

### Restant persoonsgebonden aftrek (carryforward from prior years)

- If the persoonsgebonden aftrek in a prior year exceeded the total income, the unused portion can be carried forward to subsequent years.
- The workpack should ask whether the taxpayer has unused deductions from prior years.
- Eligible whole-year fiscal partners may allocate the prior-year personal-deduction remainder in the current return. Record whose remainder it was, model traceable allocation scenarios that total 100%, and require taxpayer review in the official filing environment.

## Lijfrentepremie (annuity premium deduction)

Premiums paid for lijfrente (annuity insurance or bank savings for retirement) are deductible within limits.

### Jaarruimte (annual space)

- Jaarruimte 2025 depends on the taxpayer's income and pension accrual in 2024.
  A pensioentekort is required, and the taxpayer generally must not yet have
  reached the age that is five years above AOW age.
- Gather the 2024 income data, every 2024 UPO/factor-A or other pension-accrual
  value requested by the official tool, and any relevant net-pension or
  entrepreneur information. Pension arrangements can require different
  adjustments, so do not force every taxpayer through one handwritten formula.
- Run those facts through the Belastingdienst **Hulpmiddel Lijfrentepremie vanaf
  2016** and preserve the tool's 2025 result or screenshot as evidence. The
  agent explains the inputs and result; it does not replace the official tool
  with an invented calculator.

### Reserveringsruimte (carryback space)

- Unused jaarruimte from 2015 through 2024 may contribute to 2025
  reserveringsruimte. Ask for prior jaarruimte results and actual deductible
  lijfrente payments; do not assume that every historical year was unused.
- Use the Belastingdienst Hulpmiddel Lijfrentepremie to calculate the available
  amount. The total accumulated reserveringsruimte usable in 2025 is capped at
  EUR 42,108.
- Only premiums or deposits actually paid in 2025 can be deducted for 2025.
  Deductible lijfrentepremie is limited by the relevant available room; show
  jaarruimte, reserveringsruimte, payment evidence, and the final claimed amount
  separately for taxpayer review.

### Evidence required

- Annual statement from the lijfrente provider showing premiums paid.
- Factor A statement from the employer's pension scheme (for jaarruimte calculation).

## AOV premie (disability insurance for self-employed)

- AOV premiums for a qualifying private arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering belong
  to the **private income-provision category**, not ordinary business costs.
- The agent inventories the insurer's annual statement and policy terms. It does
  not reduce business profit by the premium. Ambiguous policy types and exact
  deductibility remain **manual review** in Mijn Belastingdienst.
- Evidence required: annual statement from the insurer.

## Partner allocation (verdeling aftrekposten)

Fiscal partners can allocate the following deductions between them in the most tax-efficient way:

### Allocatable items

- Persoonsgebonden aftrek components (alimentatie, zorgkosten, giften, etc.)
- Eigen woning: the net result can be allocated between partners
- Box 3: the joint grondslag sparen en beleggen can be allocated freely between partners

### High-income deduction-rate cap

For 2025, taxpayers with income in the highest box 1 bracket (verzamelinkomen above EUR 76,817, the start of schijf 3) do not necessarily receive a 49.50% benefit for deductions. The maximum rate for listed aftrekposten in the highest bracket is 37.48% (a tariefsaanpassing of 12.02% = 49.50% - 37.48%). This cap applies beyond own-home costs and includes, among others, persoonsgebonden aftrek, gifts, specific healthcare costs, paid partneralimentatie, own-home costs, and entrepreneur deductions where applicable.

### Allocation strategy

- Model allocation scenarios instead of assuming the highest-income partner is always best.
- The 37.48% deduction-rate cap may affect own-home costs and other listed deductions.
- The phase-out of heffingskortingen (algemene heffingskorting, arbeidskorting) also affects the optimal allocation.
- The workpack should model at least the default allocation and one alternative allocation for the taxpayer to compare, and require taxpayer review.
- Keep every scenario traceable to its inputs and do not choose a binding allocation for the taxpayer.

### Items that CANNOT be allocated

- Arbeidskorting: based on individual arbeidsinkomen
- Ondernemersaftrek: personal to the ondernemer
- MKB-winstvrijstelling: personal to the ondernemer

## Common errors to flag in the workpack

1. Claiming kinderalimentatie as deductible (it is not).
2. Forgetting the drempel for zorgkosten and giften.
3. Double-counting costs already reimbursed by insurance.
4. Not verifying ANBI status of the charity before claiming giften.
5. Using the wrong drempelinkomen (should be combined income of both partners).
6. Forgetting restant persoonsgebonden aftrek from prior years.
7. Claiming ordinary 2025 studiekosten despite the post-2021 abolition, or using
   the prestatiebeurs exception without a final DUO non-conversion notice.

## Notes

- The persoonsgebonden aftrek is allocated in order: box 1 first, then box 3, then box 2. For 2025, remember that listed deductions in the highest box 1 bracket may be capped at the 37.48% deduction rate.
- For the 2025 return, collect all supporting documents as listed in evidence-checklist.md.
- The Belastingdienst may request proof (bewijsstukken) of deductions up to 5 years after filing. The taxpayer should retain all receipts and documentation.
- Deductions reduce taxable income, not tax payable directly. The actual tax benefit depends on the marginal rate at which the deduction is applied.
- The agent applies these tables only after gathering the required facts and
  shows its classification and arithmetic. The tables support agent reasoning;
  they are not a substitute for taxpayer review in Mijn Belastingdienst.
