## Rule

Investeringsaftrek (art. 3.40 Wet IB 2001) is an extra deduction on top of
depreciation when the ondernemer invests in bedrijfsmiddelen. It comes ten laste
van de winst before the ondernemersaftrek and before the MKB-winstvrijstelling
base is determined. There are three forms: kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek
(KIA), energie-investeringsaftrek (EIA), and milieu-investeringsaftrek (MIA). The
taxpayer elects the aftrek in the aangifte. This note is canonical for the 2025
amounts.

Two neighbouring notes carry rules this one deliberately does not repeat.
`afschrijving-en-bedrijfsmiddelen-2025.md` is canonical for vermogensetikettering
(whether an asset belongs to the ondernemingsvermogen at all) and for
depreciation, including the willekeurige afschrijving that Vamil permits;
investeringsaftrek is a separate deduction that sits on top of depreciation and
never replaces it. `samenwerkingsverband-2025.md` is canonical for what a vof,
maatschap or cv means for the whole return, including the participant's share.
The position of this deduction in the ordered 2025 computation is in
`winstberekening-2025.md`.

These are reference notes for workpack preparation -- not final tax advice.

## What counts as investeren, and in which year

- **Investeren** is entering into an obligation to acquire or improve a
  bedrijfsmiddel ("het aangaan van een verplichting ter zake van de aanschaf of
  verbetering van een bedrijfsmiddel"), and also incurring voortbrengingskosten
  for a bedrijfsmiddel produced in the enterprise.
- The moment that matters is the moment a **legal obligation to pay** arises --
  for example accepting a valid offerte or concluding a purchase agreement. It is
  not the moment of delivery, of payment, or of first use. Orienting quotes,
  intentions and budget approvals are not investments.
- The investment and the resulting entitlement are reported in the aangifte
  **over the year of investeren**. Ingebruikname controls when the full
  entitlement can be effectuated; it does not automatically postpone every euro
  of aftrek to the first-use year.
- If the asset is not yet in use at the end of the boekjaar, the aftrek that may
  be taken for that year is limited to the amount actually paid in that boekjaar.
  Amounts paid can therefore support aftrek before first use. The remainder moves
  to a later year, but never later than the year of ingebruikname.
- **Improvement** costs qualify if the underlying asset itself qualifies.
  Maintenance costs generally do not count as an improvement and give no
  investeringsaftrek.
- For the KIA a bedrijfsmiddel does **not** have to be new; second-hand assets
  qualify. EIA, MIA and Vamil require an unused asset ("niet eerder gebruikt").
  A milieuvriendelijke car can still count as unused for MIA and Vamil within a
  short first-use window and a low mileage limit; those two thresholds are not
  established in this knowledge pack, so ask the taxpayer for the first-use date
  and the odometer reading at purchase and route the eligibility to manual review.

## Kleinschaligheidsinvesteringsaftrek (KIA) -- art. 3.41

Total qualifying investment per onderneming per calendar year 2025:

| Investeringsbedrag 2025            | KIA                                                     |
|------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| EUR 2,900 or less                  | EUR 0                                                   |
| more than EUR 2,900 up to EUR 70,602 | 28% of the investeringsbedrag                         |
| more than EUR 70,602 up to EUR 130,744 | EUR 19,769                                           |
| more than EUR 130,744 up to EUR 392,230 | EUR 19,769 minus 7.56% of the part above EUR 130,744 |
| more than EUR 392,230              | EUR 0                                                   |

- **Minimum per bedrijfsmiddel: EUR 450.** An asset costing less than EUR 450
  does not count toward the KIA base at all.
- In a samenwerkingsverband the bracket is determined on the total investment of
  the samenwerkingsverband; each participant gets a pro-rata share (a partnership
  complication -- route to manual review). The mechanism is set out under
  "Samenwerkingsverband: the bracket is set on the partnership total" below.

## The KIA bracket is set per onderneming

- The bracket in the table above is read off the **total qualifying investment of
  one onderneming** for the calendar year. It is not a per-asset percentage and
  it is not read off the taxpayer as a person.
- So the assets of one onderneming are added together **before** the bracket is
  chosen. A second purchase can move the yearly total into a different band and
  change the percentage that applies to the first one; never compute a KIA
  asset-by-asset and add the results up.
- The unit of aggregation changes when the ondernemer participates in a
  samenwerkingsverband -- see the next section.
- One taxpayer running **more than one onderneming** is not addressed by the
  Belastingdienst KIA guidance. Do not decide how many brackets such a taxpayer
  gets. Ask the taxpayer how many separate ondernemingen there are; if the answer
  is more than one, record the fact and route the KIA to manual review.

## Samenwerkingsverband: the bracket is set on the partnership total

In a vof, maatschap or other samenwerkingsverband (art. 3.41 lid 3 Wet IB 2001):

1. The aftrek is determined on the **total investment of the
   samenwerkingsverband**, not on each participant's own investment separately.
   Investments belonging to a participant's buitenvennootschappelijk vermogen are
   reported at the samenwerkingsverband they belong to, so they join that same
   total.
2. The resulting aftrek is then **allocated** to the participants. The default
   key is each participant's share in the (over)winst.
3. A different key is allowed only if all of the following hold: every partner
   uses the same criterion; the split rests on a reasonable basis (examples named
   by the Belastingdienst are the kapitaalsverhouding, the shares in the stille
   reserves, the shares in the winst, or equal parts); it covers every form of
   investeringsaftrek and all vennootschappelijke investeringen; the same split is
   used for the desinvesteringsbijtelling; and the participants file a **joint
   request** naming the key before the assessment of any participant becomes
   final.

Because the KIA percentage falls as the total investment rises, aggregating first
usually yields **less** than each participant would get on their own investment.
Do not present the individual figure as the answer.

Preparing a participant's share is a partnership case: record the facts (the
partnership's total investment, the participant's profit share, any agreed key,
any buitenvennootschappelijke investment) and route the share itself to manual
review. `samenwerkingsverband-2025.md` is canonical for the surrounding
partnership rules.

## Desinvesteringsbijtelling -- art. 3.47

- Applies when, within **5 years** after the start of the calendar year of the
  investment, bedrijfsmiddelen on which investeringsaftrek was claimed are
  disposed of (or otherwise deemed disposed of) for total proceeds above the
  2025 threshold of **EUR 2,900**.
- Both conditions must be met before anything is added back: the disposal falls
  inside the 5-year window **and** the combined value of the assets disposed of
  exceeds EUR 2,900. The window runs from the **start** of the calendar year in
  which the investment was made, not from the investment date.
- The bijtelling is the same percentage as the earlier investeringsaftrek applied
  to the disposal proceeds, capped so it never exceeds the earlier deduction.
- **Source discipline for this threshold:** take EUR 2,900 from art. 3.47 lid 1 as
  in force on 1 January 2025 and from the Belastingdienst desinvesteringsbijtelling
  page. Do **not** take it from hoofdstuk 8 of Fiscale informatie 2025, which
  prints EUR 2,800 -- that is the 2024 amount carried over into the 2025 chapter.
  If a workpack or a review shows EUR 2,800 for 2025, correct it to EUR 2,900.
- Where the disposal happens on cessation of the enterprise, the bijtelling raises
  the stakingswinst; `staking-2025.md` is canonical for that flow.

## Events treated as a disposal, including fictieve desinvestering

The bijtelling is not limited to a sale. Within the same 5-year window the
following also count (art. 3.47 lid 6 Wet IB 2001):

| Event | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Sale or gift of the bedrijfsmiddel | the ordinary case |
| Transfer of the asset from the ondernemingsvermogen to the **privevermogen** | the mirror image of the exclusion below: bringing an asset in gives no aftrek, taking it out triggers the bijtelling |
| Designating the asset for **verhuur** | relevant for the KIA only, not for EIA or MIA |
| The investment turning out cheaper through a subsidy or a discount | the reduction is treated as a disposal |
| **Not taken into use within 3 years** after the start of the calendar year of the investment | a fictieve desinvestering, even though nothing was sold |
| **Less than 25% of the investment paid within 12 months** while the asset is not yet in use | a fictieve desinvestering; test the payment percentage, not the intention |

The last two are the ones a taxpayer will not report on their own, because no
money came in and nothing left the enterprise. Ask about them explicitly for any
asset that was invested in but is not yet in use.

## Excluded bedrijfsmiddelen (art. 3.45 / 3.46)

Excluded for every investeringsaftrek: grond (incl. the ground under buildings),
woonhuizen and woonschepen, personenauto's not intended for beroepsvervoer,
representative vaartuigen, securities/receivables/goodwill,
vergunningen/concessies, animals, and assets mainly used abroad. Additionally
excluded for the KIA: assets intended to be made available (hoofdzakelijk, 70% or
more) to third parties, and assets under EUR 450. No investeringsaftrek for
obligations entered into with huisgenoten, close relatives, or a company in which
the taxpayer holds at least one-third.

**Assets moved in from the privevermogen are excluded.** Bedrijfsmiddelen that
the taxpayer transfers from their own privevermogen into the ondernemingsvermogen
give no investeringsaftrek, in any of its three forms. There is no investering:
no obligation towards a third party arose, the taxpayer already owned the asset.
This bites in exactly the situation a starting ondernemer finds intuitive --
bringing an existing laptop, camera or car into the business. The asset may still
be depreciated once it belongs to the ondernemingsvermogen; whether it does
belong there is decided by vermogensetikettering in
`afschrijving-en-bedrijfsmiddelen-2025.md`, which is canonical for both questions.
Moving an asset the other way, out of the ondernemingsvermogen into the
privevermogen, is a disposal for the desinvesteringsbijtelling.

Btw treatment of the investment amount: where the btw is reclaimable, use the
amount excluding btw; where there is no right to deduct btw, the amount including
btw counts. The same convention decides whether an asset clears the EUR 450 and
EUR 2,500 minimums.

## EIA, MIA, and Vamil (art. 3.42 / 3.42a / 3.31)

For new bedrijfsmiddelen on the RVO Energielijst / Milieulijst; melding to RVO is
required and the asset must not have been used before.

- **EIA 2025: 40%** of the energie-investeringen; minimum EUR 2,500 per
  bedrijfsmiddel; maximum EUR 151,000,000 of energie-investeringen per year.
- **MIA 2025: 45% / 36% / 27%** depending on the Milieulijst category; minimum
  EUR 2,500 per bedrijfsmiddel. From 2025 RVO issues the MIA-beschikking.
- **MIA/Vamil 2025 maximum: EUR 25,000,000 per taxpayer per year and per
  bedrijfsmiddel.** The RVO Milieulijst 2025 brochure states the ceiling as
  applying on both axes together -- "per belastingplichtige per jaar en per
  bedrijfsmiddel maximaal EUR 25 miljoen (tenzij in de omschrijving van het
  bedrijfsmiddel een lager maximumbedrag is opgenomen)". The RVO voorwaarden
  page states the same ceiling per bedrijfsmiddel. Do not present it as a single
  per-taxpayer annual cap: an individual Milieulijst code can carry a lower
  maximum, expressed either as an amount (possibly per unit) or as a maximum
  percentage of the investeringsbedrag, and the aftrek then applies only to that
  part of the invested amount.
  Hoofdstuk 8 of Fiscale informatie 2025 instead prints EUR 151,000,000 in its
  MIA section. That is also the 2025 EIA ceiling and conflicts with the RVO rule.
  Treat the Fiscale informatie figure as a copy error: never use EUR 151,000,000
  as the MIA/Vamil ceiling, and route any case approaching the RVO ceiling or a
  code-specific maximum to manual review.
- **Vamil 2025:** 75% of the acquisition/production cost may be depreciated at
  will (willekeurige afschrijving); the remaining 25% follows normal
  depreciation.
- Combination rules: KIA may be combined with EIA or with MIA for the same
  investment; EIA and MIA cannot both be chosen for the same bedrijfsmiddel.

## Developer instruction

1. Compute the KIA from the table above using the total qualifying investment for
   the year; never paraphrase the brackets from memory.
2. Confirm each asset meets the EUR 450 (KIA) or EUR 2,500 (EIA/MIA) minimum and
   is not on the exclusion list before including it.
3. Subtract the investeringsaftrek when determining the winst before
   ondernemersaftrek; the MKB-winstvrijstelling base is then reduced as well.
4. EIA/MIA/Vamil depend on an RVO verklaring and the annual Energielijst /
   Milieulijst; treat exact eligibility and the desinvesteringsbijtelling as
   manual-review items and direct the taxpayer to verify in Mijn Belastingdienst.
5. For every asset, ask the taxpayer four facts before deciding anything: when the
   purchase obligation was entered into, when the asset was taken into use, how
   much had been paid by the end of the boekjaar, and where it came from (bought
   from a third party, produced in the enterprise, or brought in from the
   privevermogen). Never assume any of these, and never assume zero.
6. Aggregate per onderneming before reading the bracket. If the taxpayer runs more
   than one onderneming, or participates in a samenwerkingsverband, stop before
   producing a KIA figure and route the share to manual review, citing the
   aggregation rule.
7. Read the desinvesteringsbijtelling threshold for 2025 as EUR 2,900 from this
   note. Do not source it from hoofdstuk 8 of Fiscale informatie 2025.
8. Screen for a fictieve desinvestering on any asset invested in during the last
   five calendar years that is still not in use: ask whether it was taken into use
   within 3 years of the start of its investment year, and whether at least 25% of
   the price was paid within 12 months. If either answer is no, or is not
   established, record it as a probable bijtelling and route the amount to manual
   review.
9. Assets brought in from the privevermogen produce no investeringsaftrek. If the
   taxpayer lists one, say so plainly, remove it from the KIA base, and point them
   to `afschrijving-en-bedrijfsmiddelen-2025.md` for the depreciation and
   vermogensetikettering treatment that does apply.
