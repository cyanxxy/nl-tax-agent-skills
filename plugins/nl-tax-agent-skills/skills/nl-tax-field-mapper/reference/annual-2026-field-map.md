# Resident annual 2026 preparation map

Read `../nl-tax-shared-resources/knowledge/years/2026/annual/preparation.md`
and the owning `../nl-tax-annual-return-2026/reference/annual-2026-flow.md`.
This map pairs `annual_return` with 2026, separate from resident annual 2025 and
provisional 2026. Shared `../nl-tax-shared-resources/reference/workflow-scopes.yaml`
is the identity/path contract, not a tax rule or annual form schema.

Use conceptual `annual2026.<fact>` IDs only, all with
`entry_mode: internal_routing`. Labels describe sourced preparation facts;
none claims an unreviewed annual portal field. Needed facts with missing
evidence remain explicit gaps. Do not invent a total/liability or copy the
2025 zakelijke screen inventory. Income/business actual coverage and final
status remain visible, never replace actuals with provisional estimates.

Collect household/AOW/partner periods, income and withheld tax, reconciled
business P&L/balances/capital movements and deductions, own-home and Box 2 facts,
Box 3 opening assets plus whole-year actual income and value movements, and
the 2026 own-use property benefit as an actual-return-comparison row only
(never part of the fictitious Box 3 return), deductions, credits and
prepayments. When the taxpayer's expat-ruling (30%-regeling) transitional
partial foreign tax liability choice for 2026 is unresolved, keep foreign Box 2
and Box 3 items as gaps tied to that review question rather than mapped
Dutch-reportable rows. Exact topic rules require their source; the generic
FISIN index does not establish every rule. Final Box 3 savings and debt
forfaits and annual screen review remain blockers. The owner records its
source-content, annual-schema and year-end-evidence blockers in Open questions,
Missing information and Appendix A `sections.<key>.open`, never in Appendix B;
carry each of them into this map's own gap rows when mapping.
No provisional werkelijk-rendement field is created by this workflow.

Never map identifiers/credentials. The source model/provenance/unknown rules
still apply. All maps stay draft, including after year-end until a maintainer
records real human source and exact annual schema review. No entry checklist.
