## Rule

This note is canonical for the 2025 inkomensafhankelijke bijdrage
Zorgverzekeringswet (bijdrage Zvw) owed by an ondernemer voor de
inkomstenbelasting. The legal basis is art. 43 Zorgverzekeringswet (what the
bijdrage-inkomen is and how it is capped) and art. 45 Zorgverzekeringswet (that
the bijdrage is a percentage of that bijdrage-inkomen); the 2025 percentages and
the maximumbijdrage-inkomen are fixed in hoofdstuk 5 of the Regeling
zorgverzekering and published by the Belastingdienst. The bijdrage is not part
of the inkomstenbelasting calculation: it is levied by its own aanslag. Its base
is the belastbare winst uit onderneming, so it sits at the end of the profit
chain set out in `winstberekening-2025.md`, downstream of
`investeringsaftrek.md`, `ondernemersaftrek.md` and `mkb-winstvrijstelling.md`;
because it is not deductible, it never feeds back into `winst-en-kosten.md`. The
oudedagsreserve and lijfrente facts that change the bijdrage-inkomen live in
`inkomensvoorzieningen-2025.md`.

These are reference notes for workpack preparation -- not final tax advice.

## Percentages and ceiling 2025

| Item | 2025 |
|------|------|
| Bijdrage Zvw paid by the verzekeringsplichtige through an aanslag Zvw (ondernemer, freelancer) | 5.26% |
| Werkgeversheffing Zvw paid by an inhoudingsplichtige over loon | 6.51% |
| Maximumbijdrage-inkomen | EUR 75,864 |

- The percentages are set in art. 5.4 lid 1 (werkgeversheffing) and art. 5.4
  lid 2 (verzekeringsplichtige) of the Regeling zorgverzekering; the
  maximumbijdrage-inkomen is set in art. 5.2 and art. 5.3.
- An ondernemer pays the **5.26%** rate. The 6.51% werkgeversheffing is the
  employer-side rate over loon and is never applied to winst uit onderneming;
  the Belastingdienst table lists "Ondernemer" and "Freelancer" as
  werkgeversheffing: no, pay the bijdrage yourself: yes. The 6.51% is printed
  here only so the agent does not pick the wrong percentage.
- **Do not print a maximum bijdrage in euro.** No official source publishes the
  product of 5.26% and the maximumbijdrage-inkomen for 2025. State the method
  instead: 5.26% of the bijdrage-inkomen, with the bijdrage-inkomen capped at
  EUR 75,864 and that cap reduced by any loon that already carried
  werkgeversheffing.
- Alongside this bijdrage the taxpayer also pays a nominale premie to their own
  health insurer. That is a different payment. Never net the two and never
  present the nominale premie as part of this calculation.

## Bijdrage-inkomen: what the 5.26% is applied to

- For an ondernemer the relevant component of the bijdrage-inkomen is the
  **belastbare winst uit onderneming**, determined under the rules of afdeling
  3.2 Wet IB 2001 (art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b Zorgverzekeringswet).
- Art. 3.2 Wet IB 2001 defines belastbare winst uit onderneming as the joint
  winst as ondernemer **reduced by the ondernemersaftrek and by the
  MKB-winstvrijstelling**. The Zvw base is therefore the figure **after** both,
  not the gross winst and not the winst after ondernemersaftrek alone.
- Resultaat uit overige werkzaamheden is a separate component (art. 43 lid 2
  onderdeel c), from which terbeschikkingstelling income under art. 3.91 lid 1
  onderdeel a and b and art. 3.92 Wet IB 2001 is excluded.
- Art. 43 lid 3 sets the bijdrage-inkomen at a minimum of nil. A loss year
  therefore produces no negative bijdrage and no Zvw credit.
- This base is **not** the same figure as the premiegrondslag for the
  lijfrentepremieaftrek, which uses the winst **before** the ondernemersaftrek
  (see `inkomensvoorzieningen-2025.md`). Never reuse one figure for the other.

## Shared ceiling when the taxpayer also has loon

- The maximumbijdrage-inkomen is a single ceiling across all bijdrage-inkomen,
  not one ceiling per income source. Where the bijdrage is levied by aanslag,
  the cap is reduced by the loon on which an inhoudingsplichtige already paid
  werkgeversheffing (art. 43 lid 5 Zorgverzekeringswet).
- Belastingdienst example for 2025: with loon of EUR 40,000, **EUR 35,864** of
  the maximumbijdrage-inkomen remains, so at most EUR 35,864 of the other
  income is charged. With loon of EUR 80,000 no further bijdrage is due at all.
- Ask the taxpayer for the "loon Zorgverzekeringswet" printed on each 2025
  jaaropgaaf. Do not read it off the gross salary line, do not assume it equals
  the belastbaar loon, and do not assume there was no loon at all.

## The bijdrage is not deductible

- **Not a business cost.** Art. 3.16 lid 2 onderdeel e Wet IB 2001 excludes
  levied inkomensafhankelijke bijdragen as meant in art. 43
  Zorgverzekeringswet, and any foreign scheme of the same nature and purport,
  when determining the winst. The Belastingdienst list of niet-aftrekbare kosten
  for 2025 repeats the bijdrage Zvw alongside withheld loonbelasting and premies
  volksverzekeringen.
- **Not a zorgkosten item.** Art. 6.18 Wet IB 2001 keeps the bijdrage out of the
  uitgaven voor specifieke zorgkosten, so it is not a persoonsgebonden aftrek
  item either.
- If the taxpayer's bookkeeping already charged the bijdrage to the winst, the
  winst must be corrected upwards. Record the correction and route the amount to
  manual review.
- Whether any part of the nominale premie to the health insurer is deductible is
  a separate question outside this note; route it to manual review rather than
  answering it here.

## How the bijdrage is levied and paid

- The rijksbelastingdienst levies the bijdrage (art. 48 Zorgverzekeringswet).
  For the non-wage components it is levied by way of aanslag, with the
  inkomstenbelasting rules applied correspondingly (art. 49 lid 3).
- **One return, two assessments.** The taxpayer uses the same aangifte
  inkomstenbelasting; when the return is filed online, the bijdrage Zvw is
  calculated automatically from the figures already in it. After the return the
  taxpayer receives two separate assessments: one for the inkomstenbelasting and
  premie volksverzekeringen, and a separate one for the bijdrage Zvw.
- Consequence for the workpack: there is no separate Zvw form and no separate
  Zvw entry screen. Do not create a field-map row asking the taxpayer to type a
  bijdrage Zvw amount anywhere.
- Cash-flow consequence: the taxpayer should expect a second payment obligation
  on top of the income-tax assessment. Present the method, never a predicted
  assessment amount.
- You (the taxpayer) read both assessments in Mijn Belastingdienst or on the
  paper copies. This plugin never opens the portal, never logs in and never
  retrieves an assessment.

## Teruggaaf of over-withheld bijdrage

- This route concerns bijdrage Zvw that was **withheld** during a calendar year,
  typically because loon, pensioen and uitkering from more than one withholding
  agent together exceeded the maximumbijdrage-inkomen.
- The Belastingdienst arranges the refund from its own records and the taxpayer
  does not have to apply for it. The refund is paid in **March or April of the
  following calendar year**, so an over-withholding in 2025 is refunded during
  2026.
- The Belastingdienst page describing this refund does not discuss winst uit
  onderneming. Do not promise this route to an ondernemer whose bijdrage is
  levied by aanslag; for that taxpayer any correction runs through the aanslag
  itself. Route the question to manual review.

## Exceptions: zeevarenden, deelvissers and other special cases

- Art. 5.4 lid 3 of the Regeling zorgverzekering sets a separate, nil percentage
  for bijdrage-inkomen earned from work aboard a zeeschip, under the conditions
  stated in that lid. The reviewed material records that nil percentage for
  2026; the 2025 value is not established here, so **do not state a 2025
  percentage for this case**.
- The Belastingdienst exception list for 2025 also covers militairen,
  deelvissers whose care costs are borne for 30% or more by another party, a
  correction where premiums were paid abroad, and a reduction where the
  taxpayer was insured for only part of the year.
- Any taxpayer falling in one of these categories is manual review. Record the
  facts and the question; do not compute a bijdrage for them.

## Oudedagsreserve release and the bijdrage-inkomen

- Art. 43 lid 2 onderdeel b Zorgverzekeringswet excludes from the
  bijdrage-inkomen the amount added to the winst under old art. 3.70 lid 2
  Wet IB 2001 for a decrease under old art. 3.70 lid 1 onderdeel a -- that is,
  the **voluntary** release of an oudedagsreserve matched by a lijfrente
  purchase in the same year.
- A **mandatory** release is not excluded. Where the reserve exceeds the
  ondernemingsvermogen at the end of the year and the taxpayer staked the
  business, reached AOW-leeftijd on 1 January, or failed the urencriterium in
  both this and the preceding calendar year, the release raises the winst and
  therefore raises the bijdrage-inkomen.
- The classification drives the Zvw outcome, so record which release occurred
  before anything is calculated. The overgangsrecht itself is in
  `inkomensvoorzieningen-2025.md` and the balance-sheet side in
  `winst-en-kosten.md`; every FOR movement stays manual review.

## Developer instruction

1. Read the percentages and the maximumbijdrage-inkomen from the table above.
   Never restate them from memory. Every euro amount and percentage in this note
   comes from the Belastingdienst rates pages and hoofdstuk 5 of the Regeling
   zorgverzekering; the articles of the Zorgverzekeringswet are named as legal
   basis only and must never be used as the source of an amount.
2. Build the base in this order: winst uit onderneming, minus investeringsaftrek
   (`investeringsaftrek.md`), minus ondernemersaftrek (`ondernemersaftrek.md`),
   minus the MKB-winstvrijstelling (`mkb-winstvrijstelling.md`). The result is
   the belastbare winst uit onderneming and it is the Zvw base -- the final line
   of the chain in `winstberekening-2025.md`, not an earlier one. Show that line
   explicitly in the workpack so the base is traceable.
3. Ask the taxpayer whether they also had loon, pensioen or an uitkering in
   2025 and, for each, ask for the "loon Zorgverzekeringswet" from the
   jaaropgaaf. Never assume there was none and never derive it from the gross
   salary. If the taxpayer cannot produce the figure, mark the ceiling
   calculation manual review.
4. Present the method, not a prediction. Do not print a maximum bijdrage in euro
   and do not tell the taxpayer what the aanslag Zvw will say.
5. Tell the taxpayer plainly that two separate assessments follow the 2025
   return and that the second one is the bijdrage Zvw. You (the taxpayer) open
   and check both yourself; the plugin never opens Mijn Belastingdienst.
6. Never deduct the bijdrage: not from the winst and not as a zorgkosten item.
   If the bookkeeping already deducted it, flag the correction and route it to
   manual review.
7. Ask whether the taxpayer worked aboard a zeeschip, was a deelvisser, was a
   militair, paid premiums abroad, or was insured for only part of 2025. Any yes
   is manual review; do not state a 2025 percentage for those cases.
8. If an oudedagsreserve decreased during 2025, ask whether the release was
   matched by a lijfrente purchase (voluntary) or was mandatory, and record the
   answer. Do not assume either way.
9. Collect only the amounts and dates the calculation needs. Do not record a
   BSN, an aanslagnummer or a bank account number from any jaaropgaaf or
   assessment.
10. Recheck the percentages and the maximumbijdrage-inkomen before the 2026
    season; both are reset each calendar year by ministerial regulation.
