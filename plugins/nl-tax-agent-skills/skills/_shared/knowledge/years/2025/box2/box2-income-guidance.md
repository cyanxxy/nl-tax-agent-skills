## Rule

Income from a substantial interest can include regular benefits and disposal
benefits. Keep both categories separate in the workpack and preserve the source
documents used for each amount.

## Regular benefits

Regular benefits include dividends from the company in which the taxpayer has a
substantial interest. Dutch dividend withholding tax can be credited in the tax
calculation when it was withheld on the Box 2 income.

## Disposal benefits

Disposal benefits include profit on a sale or transfer of shares. The disposal
benefit is the official transfer price minus the acquisition price. Treat the
official transfer price as the net price after sale or transfer costs; if only
gross proceeds are available, subtract those costs once to derive the transfer
price before calculating the benefit.

## Developer instruction

Ask for dividend statements, withholding evidence, share-sale agreements, and
acquisition-price support where relevant. Keep the output as a manual
preparation workpack; do not handle portal navigation or
submission.
