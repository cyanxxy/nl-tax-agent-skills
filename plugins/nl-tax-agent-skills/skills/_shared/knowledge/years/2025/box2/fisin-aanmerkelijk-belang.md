## Rule

For 2025 annual-return workpacks, use the FISIN substantial-interest doctrine
as the year-specific reference for Box 2 review questions and allocation notes.

## Substantial interest threshold

A substantial interest generally exists at a holding threshold of 5%, assessed
together with the taxpayer's fiscal partner where applicable.

## Fiscal partner allocation

Full-year fiscal partners may allocate Box 2 income between them in any split
as long as the total allocation is 100%.

## Excessive borrowing

Excessive borrowing above EUR 500,000 from the taxpayer's own BV can create a
fictitious regular benefit. Flag this as a manual review item and collect loan
balance evidence before using any amount in the workpack.
