## Rule

For 2026 provisional-assessment workpacks, use the FISIN substantial-interest
doctrine as the year-specific reference for Box 2 review questions and
allocation notes.

## Substantial interest threshold

A substantial interest generally exists at a holding threshold of 5%, assessed
together with the taxpayer's fiscal partner where applicable.

## Income, rates, and withholding

Box 2 income can include regular benefits such as dividends, fictitious regular
benefits from excessive borrowing, and disposal benefits such as share-sale
profit. For 2026, taxable Box 2 income is taxed at 24.5% up to EUR 68,843 and
31% above that amount. Dutch dividend withholding tax withheld on Box 2 income
can be credited as withholding tax.

## Fiscal partner allocation

Full-year fiscal partners may allocate Box 2 income between them in any split
as long as the total allocation is 100%.

## Excessive borrowing

If the taxpayer and fiscal partner together have borrowed more than EUR 500,000
from the own BV on 31 December 2026, the amount above the threshold can be
taxed as a fictitious regular benefit in Box 2. The threshold can be raised by
fictitious regular benefits from earlier years and reduced by repayments, with
a minimum threshold of EUR 500,000. Home-loan exceptions can apply.

Flag own-BV loan balances as manual-review data before using any fictitious
regular benefit amount in a provisional workpack.
