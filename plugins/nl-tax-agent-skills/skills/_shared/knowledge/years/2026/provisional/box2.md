## Rule

For 2026 provisional-assessment workpacks, estimate Box 2 income from regular
benefits and disposal benefits separately, then apply the 2026 Box 2 rate
brackets.

## 2026 rate bracket

- 24.5% on Box 2 income up to and including EUR 68,843
- 31% on Box 2 income above EUR 68,843

## Income categories

Regular benefits include dividends. Disposal benefits include share-sale profit,
calculated as the official transfer price minus acquisition price. Treat the
official transfer price as the net price after sale or transfer costs; if only
gross proceeds are available, subtract those costs once to derive the transfer
price before calculating the estimated benefit. Dutch dividend withholding tax
can be credited where withholding evidence supports it.

## Excessive borrowing marker

If the taxpayer and fiscal partner together have borrowed more than EUR 500,000
from the own BV on 31 December 2026, the amount above the threshold can be a
fictitious regular benefit in Box 2. The threshold can be adjusted by earlier
fictitious regular benefits, repayments, and home-loan exceptions, so the
helper must flag this for manual review before using an amount.

## Developer instruction

Use this note for manual provisional workpacks only. Ask for dividend estimates,
share-transfer evidence, acquisition-price support, and withholding evidence.
Do not automate portal access, DigiD use, or submission.
