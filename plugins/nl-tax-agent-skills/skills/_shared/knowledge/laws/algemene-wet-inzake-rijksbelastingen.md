## Rule

Article 52 of the Algemene wet inzake rijksbelastingen (AWR) is the legal basis
for the fiscal administration and retention duties relevant to entrepreneurs.
Paragraphs 1 and 2 establish who must keep an administration and what that
administration must show. Paragraph 4 requires the relevant books, records, and
other data carriers to be retained for seven years.

The official Belastingdienst entrepreneur guidance applies this as a seven-year
retention duty for the basic business administration. Topic-specific guidance
may require a longer period for particular records, including records concerning
immovable property; use the reviewed entrepreneur note for those operational
details.

## Developer instruction

- Cite `law_awr_artikel_52` when a workpack states the general business
  administration or seven-year retention duty.
- Keep the legal duty separate from evidence-completeness checks and from any
  topic-specific longer period.
- Do not attribute the general retention duty to the Uitvoeringsregeling
  inkomstenbelasting 2001.
